Layar

A .NET port of Laya: typed decisions (choice, score, noul) answered over state in one forward pass — no text generation, nothing to hallucinate.

Layar.Onnx

ONNX Runtime. Portable, wide execution-provider support.

Layar.TorchSharp

libtorch, via a torch.jit.traced module — not a hand-ported architecture.
